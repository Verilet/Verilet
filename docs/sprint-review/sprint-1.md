## Actions to Stop Doing

1. **Stop coordinating meeting times through Discord messages.**  
   _Explanation:_ At the start of the sprint, we all posted our schedules into Discord, which got messy. Trying to cross-reference class schedules over text took way too long. We should stop doing this and just use When2meet or a shared calendar so we can find meeting times without the excessive mental load.

2. **Stop trying to reach team members on GrepThink instead of Discord.**  
   _Explanation:_ During the first few days, we had delays reaching people because some members weren't checking GrepThink or Discord regularly. We should stop splitting our communication across different platforms and keep all project messages, questions, and announcements in our Discord server.

---

## Actions to Start Doing

1. **Start creating standard Jira ticket templates with clear acceptance criteria.**  
   _Explanation:_ A lot of our Sprint 1 tickets were just titles with empty descriptions and no clear requirements. We should make a simple template for future tickets that spells out the acceptance criteria so whoever picks up the ticket knows exactly what needs to be finished.

2. **Start estimating ideal work hours on tickets during sprint planning.**  
   _Explanation:_ For Sprint 1, our tickets had "None" entered for work estimates in Jira. We should start estimating ideal hours for each ticket during sprint planning so our Scrum Master can properly track our progress and velocity.

3. **Start holding scheduled recurring standups instead of spontaneous check-ins.**  
   _Explanation:_ In Sprint 1, our meetings and check-ins were mostly spontaneous or organized on short notice over Discord. Setting a fixed, recurring day and time for standups will make attendance much more consistent and allow everyone to plan their week around it.

---

## Actions to Keep Doing

1. **Keep sharing class notes and lecture recordings.**  
   _Explanation:_ Whenever someone had a class conflict or couldn't make lecture, other teammates were quick to share notes or recording links. This made sure nobody fell behind.

2. **Keep replying quickly on Discord.**  
   _Explanation:_ People were pretty responsive to general questions and polls in Discord, which kept our initial setup moving forward without long delays.
